# 🛒 Maven Market Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 1. Project Title 

**Maven Market: Retail Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales, product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and store operations to generate meaningful business insights.

## 2. Short Description 

The **Maven Market Dashboard** is a retail analytics project developed to explore sales performance, product trends, customer behavior, and store-level results. It helps transform raw retail data into actionable insights, enabling users to evaluate business performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 3. Tech Stack

- **📊 Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **📂 Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data storage and source files, depending on the dataset format.
- **🔄 Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation, if used.
- **🧠 DAX** – Calculated measures and key performance indicators, if used.
- **📁 File Formats** – `.pbix` for the Power BI project and image files for dashboard previews.

## 4. Data Source

**Source:** Maven Market retail dataset.

The dataset is used to explore retail transactions and related business information. Depending on the tables included, it may contain details about sales, products, customers, stores, and transaction dates.

## 5. Features / Highlights

### Business Problem

Retail businesses need to understand sales performance, product demand, and customer purchasing behavior to make effective business decisions. Analyzing large amounts of retail data manually can make it difficult to identify patterns and evaluate performance.

### Goal of the Dashboard

The goal is to transform retail data into clear and interactive visualizations that help users analyze sales performance, compare products, evaluate store results, and identify meaningful business trends.

### Walkthrough of Key Visuals

- **Sales KPIs:** Summarize key sales and performance metrics available in the dashboard.
- **Product Analysis:** Explore product performance and sales contribution.
- **Customer Analysis:** Understand customer purchasing patterns using available data.
- **Store Performance:** Compare results across stores or locations, if included.
- **Time-Based Analysis:** Explore sales trends over different periods, if date information is available.
- **Interactive Filters:** Enable users to analyze specific products, stores, or time periods.

### Business Impact & Insights

- **Sales Optimization:** Identify products and categories contributing to sales.
- **Customer Understanding:** Explore purchasing patterns to support marketing decisions.
- **Store Performance:** Compare business performance across locations.
- **Strategic Planning:** Use data-driven insights to support retail operations and business growth.
